[H1]Ardas[\\H1][PARAGRAPH:2]The term Arda refers to the divine power that a god is able to grant to his priests and to his angels.[PARAGRAPH]In the Age of Dragons, the gods could grant a full strength arda to anyone they wished, even those fighting deep in enemy territory. The Compact changed things. The gods are now only permitted to extend an arda to their servants in Erebus based on the strength of the faith manifested by their nearby mortal worshippers. [PARAGRAPH]Alliances and enmities between the gods mean that faith in the caster's own religion is not the only relevant factor, as friendly gods may allow the caster's gods some strength and believers in the god's enemies may sheild their neighbors against his attacks. \r\n\r\n[PARAGRAPH] In this modmod the Strength of an Arda is represented by a range of promotion that primarily influence the chance that spells will miscast. Units with a religion and the Divine promotion will have these Arda level promotions autoomatically swapped out based on a number of factors. These include the state religion of the caster's owner, the state religion of the player who controlls the territory on which he unit is located, the preferences of the player's AI towards each religion, the religions present in the nearest city, the religious buildings in the nearest city, the distances from various holy cities, and by Mana or unique features present on the caster's tile or on adjacent tiles.\r\n\r\n\r\n[PARAGRAPH]All priests have their Ardas weakened by the presence of the Children of the One or of the Matronae in the world, especially in cities nearby. Once the Matronae influence is strong enough, priests in the vicinity of Matronae cities or of Apostates will abdondon their leaders and become Apostates themselves.
