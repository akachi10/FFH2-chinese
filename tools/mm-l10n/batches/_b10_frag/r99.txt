[H1]Hell Terrain[\\H1][PARAGRAPH:2]Hell Terrain represents the corruption which can spread across Erebus as it falls under the influence of Evil. It is very much tied to the [LINK=CONCEPT_ARMAGEDDON_COUNTER]Armageddon Counter[\\LINK], but its effects are local instead of global. [NEWLINE][NEWLINE]The [LINK=GAMEOPTION_NO_PLOT_COUNTER]No Hell Terrain[\\LINK] game option prevents the spread of hell entirely. (The [LINK=FEATURE_HALLOWED_GROUND]Hallowed Ground[\\LINK] improvement prevents the spread of hell terrain into the tile, but is only used for scenarios. ) Otherwise, Hell Terrain always spreads quickly into territory held by the [LINK=CIVILIZATION_INFERNAL]Infernals[\\LINK], and recedes from territory held by the [LINK=CIVILIZATION_MERCURIANS]Mercurians[\\LINK]. If an adjacent tile (or one the other side of a [LINK=IMPROVEMENT_PORTAL]Portal[\\LINK] ) is already corrupted, then Hell may spread into lands whose owner has [LINK=RELIGION_THE_ASHEN_VEIL]The Ashen Veil[\\LINK] as a State Religion when the AC more than 10, into lands held by other Evil leaders once the AC passes 25, into the lands of Neutral leaders once it passes 50, and into the lands of Good leaders once it passes 90. It recedes faster from Good lands, slower from Evil lands, and not at all from lands held by The Ashen Veil. [NEWLINE][NEWLINE]The appearance of [LINK=IMPROVEMENT_HELLFIRE]Hellfire[\\LINK], a [LINK=IMPROVEMENT_NECROTOTEM]Necrototem[\\LINK], or [LINK=FEATURE_TORMENTED_SOULS]Tormented Souls[\\LINK] on a tile turns it into Hell Terrain instantly, and keeps it that way as long as it remains. [NEWLINE][NEWLINE]An [LINK=IMPROVEMENT_MANA_ENTROPY]Entropy Node[\\LINK] may flair up and turn the surrounding tiles into Hell. [NEWLINE][NEWLINE]Units with the [LINK=PROMOTION_UNHOLY_TAINT]Unholy Taint[\\LINK], [LINK=PROMOTION_DEMON]Demon[\\LINK], [LINK=PROMOTION_POSSESSED]Demon Possessed[\\LINK], or [LINK=PROMOTION_KANNAS_WHIP]Kanna's Whip[\\LINK] promotions may spread it to the tile they occupy regardless of how pure or tainted adjacent tiles are. [NEWLINE][NEWLINE][NEWLINE]Units with the [LINK=PROMOTION_ANGEL]Angel[\\LINK], [LINK=PROMOTION_BLESSED]Blessed[\\LINK], or [LINK=PROMOTION_CROWN_OF_BRILLANCE]Crown of Brilliance[\\LINK] promotions may help to slow or reverse the spread of hell. [NEWLINE][NEWLINE]The [LINK=SPELL_SANCTIFY]Sanctify[\\LINK] spell and the [LINK=BUILDING_CHANCEL_OF_GUARDIANS]Chancel of the Guardians[\\LINK] building can instantly heal hell terrain.
